## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one small illustrated developer avatar for the top of a software engineer's portfolio. Create a polished, friendly 3D cartoon bust, with deliberately simplified non-photorealistic facial proportions: warm medium-brown skin, short fluffy dark hair, round dark eyeglasses, a simple short dark beard, and a forest-green crewneck shirt. This is a fictional avatar, not a recognizable photographic portrait or an exact likeness of a real person. Mature and approachable rather than childlike. Soft matte clay materials, clean sculpted shapes, gentle studio lighting, pale-lime edge highlights. Front-facing with a slight friendly head tilt. Square composition, entire head and shoulders visible, generous safe margin for a circular crop; the face should read clearly at 80 pixels. Genuinely transparent background. No realistic skin texture, no photographic elements, no text, no logos, no watermark, no extra objects or background.
